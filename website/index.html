Three keystrokes between meeting a word and knowing it

No switching apps, no retyping, no copy-paste round trip through a translation site.

  1. Select

    Select a word in any app

    A browser, a PDF, your mail, a code comment. The clipboard is never touched, so whatever you had copied stays copied.

  2. Capture

    Press ⌥⌘C

    A HUD confirms on screen and the word lands in your list with its definition already filled in. For text you can't select — images, PDFs, a screenshot someone sent you — ⌥⌘O recognizes it on device instead.

  3. Review

    Flip the card when it comes back

    Know it and the interval stretches; don't and it resets to tomorrow. A daily notification tells you when something is due.

Built-in dictionary

About 50,000 entries ship inside the app, with phonetics and both Chinese and English glosses. Inflected forms resolve to their base form, so brought finds bring. Bring your own dictionary and it stacks on top as an extra source.
