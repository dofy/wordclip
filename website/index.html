Three keystrokes between meeting a word and knowing it

No switching apps, no retyping, no copy-paste round trip through a translation site.

  1. Select

    Select a word in any app

    A browser, a PDF, your mail, a code comment. The clipboard is never touched, so whatever you had copied stays copied.

  2. Capture

    Press ⌥⌘C

    A HUD confirms on screen and the word lands in your list with its definition already filled in. For text you can't select — images, PDFs, a screenshot someone sent you — ⌥⌘O recognizes it on device instead.

  3. Review

    Flip the card when it comes back

    Know it and the interval stretches; don't and it resets to tomorrow. A daily notification tells you when something is due.

A dictionary you can't reach offline isn't a dictionary.

The words ship with the app.

◎

Built-in dictionary

About 50,000 entries from ECDICT, with phonetics and both Chinese and English glosses, in the bundle. Inflected forms resolve to their base form, so brought finds bring. Import your own CSV or ECDICT-shaped SQLite to stack more definition sources on top.

~50,000 entries · offline · MIT-licensed data
◍

Word packs

Twelve ready-made sets — graded ones for Zhongkao, Gaokao, CET-4 and 6, postgraduate exams, TOEFL, IELTS and GRE, and topical ones for daily life, travel, tech and business. Free, offline, and kept in their own group so they never mix with the words you saved yourself.

12 packs · import and remove at will

Built for the way you actually read.

Fast where it has to be, quiet everywhere else, and native on every screen you own.

SM-2 spaced repetition

The interval grows as you keep knowing a word and resets when you don't. Behind a protocol, so a move to FSRS later is a small change.

OCR capture

Drag out any region of the screen. Recognition runs on device through the system's Vision framework — nothing is uploaded.

Keyboard all the way down

Navigate, search, add, speak, favorite, repair and delete without reaching for the trackpad. ⌘/ shows the whole cheat sheet.

One codebase, three platforms

SwiftUI and SwiftData across macOS, iOS and iPadOS, with a shared Swift package carrying the dictionary and the review engine.

Buy it once. That's the whole arrangement.

One purchase, unlocked for life — no subscription, no auto-renewal. Capturing words, the offline dictionary, the word list, review, stats and exporting a backup are free forever; OCR capture and importing a vocabulary are Pro. Nothing is measured, nothing is uploaded, and the app works with the network off.
